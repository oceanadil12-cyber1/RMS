## The basics
- Name: RMS, a restaurant, bar and live-entertainment venue.
- Address: 2266 Wilton Drive, Wilton Manors, FL 33305 (the heart of Wilton Manors, South Florida).
- Phone: (954) 565-2526
- Email: info@example.com
- Instagram: @rms (lineup changes and special events are posted there).
- Open every day, Monday to Sunday, 11:00 AM to 2:00 AM (that's the bar's hours).
- Kitchen hours for dining in aren't listed (the kitchen is "open late"); online food orders follow the pickup hours in the ordering section. For late-night food, suggest calling to check.
- Parking: street parking and public lots nearby.
- Open since 1997 (29 years). An LGBTQ+ community cornerstone; everyone is welcome.
- No cover charge for the weekly shows (every night).

## Spaces
- Main Bar: large, social; home of the Main Stage (DJs, dance parties, karaoke, live music).
- Lounge: intimate room with its own stage for up-close drag, comedy and live music.
- Outdoor Patio: open-air dining with a tropical feel.

## Food and ordering
- Scratch kitchen: burgers, wings, sandwiches, wraps, salads, flatbreads, Chinese-style mains, tacos, desserts.
- Popular dishes: Chicken Wings (guests love them with BBQ, Hot or Mild sauce), The Champ Burger, Mexican Street Tacos, Mucho Nachos and flatbreads.
- Light bites and snacks: Edamame, salads, flatbreads and appetizers.
- Shareable plates: Mucho Nachos, Chicken Wings (8 or 12), and appetizers to share.
- Online ordering for pickup or delivery is on the Menu page (/menu.html). Guests can also order right here in the chat: just say what you'd like and the assistant adds it to your order.
- Payment is cash on delivery: pay with cash when the food arrives or is picked up.
- Pickup: curbside by the front door (look for the sign, call when you arrive) or at the host stand on the patio. Have your order number and name ready.
- Delivery: available to select ZIP codes. The guest enters their delivery address at checkout. Delivery fee and minimum order apply (see the ordering details on /menu.html).
- How to order in the chat: tell the assistant what you want (e.g. "I'll have 12 wings with BBQ sauce and a burger"), choose your size and options when asked, then tap "Review order" to pick up or delivery and place the order.
- Food allergies or dietary needs: tell your server before ordering; for special requests in advance, call the bar. The assistant can note requests like "no onions" or "gluten-free bun" in the order.
- Gluten-free items say "Gluten free" in their menu description.
- Any changes or substitutions are subject to additional charges, and extra dressings and sauces cost extra.
- In the restaurant, a 3% surcharge is added to credit card transactions (debit cards excluded).
- Non-alcoholic Zero Alc-Tails are available at the bar.
- Drinks are ordered at the bar and are not sold online.

## Happy hour and drink deals
- Happy Hour every day 4:00 PM to 6:45 PM (food specials are on the Menu page).
- 2-for-1 drinks until 8 PM, plus nightly drink specials that go with the shows (see the lineup). 2-4-1 does not apply to the signature cocktails on the menu.

## Reservations
- Walk-ins are always welcome; a reservation guarantees a table.
- Book with the reservation form on the Contact page (/contact.html#reserve) or call (954) 565-2526.
- Reservation requests are confirmed by the team by phone or email, not instantly.
- Weekend reservations: book about 48 hours ahead.
- Parties of 8 or more: please call directly.
- Areas you can ask for: Main Bar, Lounge or Outdoor Patio.
- Tuesday's The Groove Session is "dinner and a show": reservations recommended.

## Private events (/private-events.html)
- Birthdays, corporate events, anniversaries, engagements, graduations, showers and more.
- Add-ons: drag performers, DJs, live music, custom catering (passed appetizers to plated dinners), open bar / hosted bar / cash bar, custom cocktail menus, bottle service, decorations.
- Spaces: Lounge (30-60 guests), Outdoor Patio (40-80 guests), full venue buyout (up to 300 guests).
- Lounge Mondays: the Lounge is closed to the public on Mondays and can be booked privately.
- Packages (starting prices; the events team quotes the final price):
  - Bronze from $500: reserved seating area, appetizer platter, cash bar, up to 20 guests, dedicated server.
  - Gold from $1,500 (most popular): private Lounge area, custom food menu, 2-hour open bar, up to 60 guests, DJ or live entertainment, custom decorations.
  - Platinum from $5,000: full venue buyout, custom plated menu, 4-hour premium bar, up to 300 guests, full entertainment lineup, event coordinator.
- To plan one: the contact form (/contact.html, subject "Private Event") or call (954) 565-2526.
